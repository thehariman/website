Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Thiranex internship completely free?

Yes, applying and completing the Thiranex internship is 100% free. You work on real-world projects and gain skills without any upfront cost. A administrative processing fee for the MSME-verified certificate applies only after you successfully complete your tasks and request your final credentials.

Are Thiranex internships virtual or offline?

All Thiranex internships are 100% virtual (remote). This allows students from Bangalore, Chennai, Mumbai, Delhi, and rural areas across India to gain industry experience without relocating.

Can I do this internship along with my college studies?

Absolutely! Our programs are self-paced and designed to be flexible. You can manage your tasks alongside your B.Tech, BCA, or MCA coursework at your convenience.

What is the selection criteria for the 2026 batch?

We value passion and the willingness to learn. Any student currently enrolled in a degree program (UG/PG) or recent graduates looking to build their portfolio can apply. No prior experience is required as we provide comprehensive project task guides.

How soon will I receive my Offer Letter?

Once you submit your registration, our system processes it instantly. You will be able to login to your dashboard and download your official Offer Letter within 24-48 hours after admin verification.
